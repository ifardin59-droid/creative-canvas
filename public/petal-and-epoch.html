The Atelier Process

5 Steps of Reverent Preservation

From the moment you package your flowers to the final diamond-buffed delivery, we treat every petal with sacred care.

01

Thermal Kit & Dispatch

We ship you an insulated packaging container with petal stabilizers to safely send fresh blooms to our studio.

02

Gentle Dehydration

Blooms undergo 2 to 3 weeks of micro-silica dehydration, locking in natural petal shape and deep botanical color.

03

Composition Approval

Our head artisan creates a dry mock-up arrangement. You review and approve the exact layout before pouring begins.

04

Vacuum Chamber Cast

Optical-grade resin is poured in micro-layers inside jeweler's pressure chambers to eliminate every bubble.

05

Diamond Polishing

Hand-sanded through 9 grit grades and diamond-buffed for crystal clarity. Delivered in velvet heirloom packaging.

Questions & Guidance

Preservation Knowledge

Everything you need to know about preparing blooms, resin longevity, and shipping.

Which flowers preserve best in resin? expand_more

Hardier blooms like roses, ranunculus, spray roses, baby's breath, eucalyptus, marigolds, and hydrangea hold their three-dimensional shape and vibrancy exceptionally well. Thinner white petals or delicate lilies turn slightly translucent in resin, creating a romantic vintage effect.

How do I safely send my fresh celebration flowers without wilting? expand_more

Once you book your slot, we dispatch our temperature-shielded packing kit. Simply wrap the stems in damp paper towels sealed with cling film, place delicate blooms in bubble padding, and send via 24-hour express courier. Never seal the flowers in an airtight plastic bag without moisture towels.

Will the resin turn yellow over time? expand_more

We exclusively cast with German archival, optical-grade epoxy fortified with proprietary HALS UV-stabilizers. Unlike standard craft resins that degrade in sunlight within months, our keepsakes come with a 10-Year Zero-Yellowing Studio Guarantee under normal indoor display conditions.

What if some petals are already partially dried or years old? expand_more

Yes! We regularly accept sentimental flowers from past anniversaries or memorial ceremonies from years ago. Our artisans gently reinforce brittle petals with specialized botanical clear sealants before vacuum encapsulation.

Do I get to approve the arrangement layout before casting? expand_more

Always. Once your blooms are dried and inspected, our artisan creates a dry mock-up inside your chosen mold and sends high-resolution photos and video via WhatsApp or email. Resin is only poured after your full sign-off.

Limited Studio Capacity

Don't let a once-in-a-lifetime bloom fade into a photograph.

Because each keepsake requires 4 to 6 weeks of dedicated artisanal curing and hand-polishing, our studio accepts only 25 custom bouquet commissions per month.
